About

I'm a product marketer in banking and finance, currently an Assistant Vice President in Group Marketing at OCBC bank — one of Singapore's largest financial institutions.

With a double major in Politics, Law & Economics and Marketing I partner with cross-functional teams to translate complex financial products into clear, compelling experiences. Most recently, I led the launch of OCBC MyOwn Account — Singapore's first bank account for youths aged 7–15 — acquiring over 35,000 customers in six months and becoming the fastest-growing account across all customer segments.

I hold a Bachelor of Social Science from Singapore Management University, with international exposure from Erasmus University Rotterdam. Outside of work, I represented Singapore at the 29th SEA Games as a member of the National Swimming Team, placing 5th in the 10km Open Water Swimming — and have been recognised for leadership across student life, community impact, and corporate finance.
